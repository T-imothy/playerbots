Shared battleground objective recovery, 2026-10-15

The Oct 14 integration enabled objective recovery only under MANGOSBOT_ZERO.
Its common WSG/AB/AV behavior now uses a battleground admission helper rather
than an expansion guard. Classic, TBC and Wrath register the same combat and
dead-engine hooks, noncombat objective movement priority, death reset and
accepted-cast/channel preservation. Wrath random queues resolve the actual
battleground before admission. Arenas, EY, IC and other expansion-only maps
retain their existing planners and registration.

AB/AV ownership refresh remains bounded to ten seconds, keeps the bot's
native job, restores the old objective when native selection fails and
returns no new movement for an identical objective. No periodic refresh is
performed while loading, dead, outside the world, casting or outside an
active match. This bound is for objective reselection, not NPC engagement.
After death the old route/objective is cleared once and the next live
selection can run immediately. Existing WSG deterministic flag decisions
remain authoritative. No leader or controller is required by these hooks.

The existing unmarked AV NPC engagement fix is already shared across all
three defines and remains unchanged. It admits only enemy NPCs actually
fighting nearby members of the same active native BG raid. It preserves
skull precedence, native hostility, CC, team, instance and distance checks.

This is a shared movement/recovery correction, not a complete independent AV
planner redesign or a claim that all reported idling is fixed. Native node,
captain, general and mine objectives and walking paths remain in use.
No commander NPC script, quest SQL, raid routine, addon protocol, ownership
check, teleport, configuration or native spell cooldown is changed here.

The historical classic_bg_recovery_regression.py filename is retained; its
fixtures now execute the actual shared helper, strategy trigger bodies,
objective refresh and cast/death admission under ZERO, ONE and TWO defines.
They cover inactive/loading/dead refusal, failed selection retention, stable
jobs, duplicate death resets, existing WSG delegation, Wrath random AV and
exclusion of other BGs. Native unmarked-NPC and Warsong suites run alongside
it. Full builds and deployed runtime receipts remain separate from tests.
