Classic BG and raid adapters (2026-10-14)

Scope: Classic only. WSG, AB, AV and six instance raids outside Molten Core:
Onyxia, BWL, ZG, AQ20, AQ40, Naxxramas. World bosses and UBRS are not part of
this package. TBC/Wrath registration does not activate these new handlers.

Donor: mod-playerbots, GPL v2 or later, revision
79bd428115c8f33b74b13de23b133d49c3f75c60.
Compared src/Ai/Base/Strategy/BattlegroundStrategy.cpp and src/Ai/Raid/{Ony,
Bwl,Aq20,Naxx}, including their action, trigger and multiplier implementations.
This is a native adaptation of useful gaps, not a wholesale engine transplant.
The audited donor has no dedicated ZG/AQ40 module and its Naxx rules are Wrath
rules. Its commented-out Patchwerk rules are not advertised as active tactics.

BG mapping
----------
Common donor objective/death/combat/ended hooks: added Classic combat and dead
engine registration; capture/ended checks remain available in combat. AB/AV
objectives refresh at most every ten seconds without rerolling native jobs.
Native failed selections retain a usable previous objective. An active native
cast/banner channel is preserved; refresh is not an engagement delay.
WSG: retained native deterministic flag-state/team-job planner, flag carrier
pursuit/protection, buffs and captures. AB: retained native node admission,
banner interaction, routes and jobs. AV: retained native nodes, captains,
generals, mines and routes; the baseline unmarked allied-NPC engagement fix
remains included. Wrath-only BGs, vehicles and queues are not imported.

Raid mapping
------------
Onyxia: retained native deep-breath spell-target geometry, whelp handling,
fireball spread and flank/tail avoidance. Added egg-entrance exclusion and an
explicit emergency escape trigger, verified against native egg clusters;
native learned Fear Ward support. No Wrath phase HP thresholds imported.

BWL: retained native Razorgore phase admission, add priority, suppression
devices, Burning Adrenaline, Firemaw stack cover/taunts, Ebonroc tank swaps,
Flamegor/Chromaggus Tranq/dispels, Hourglass Sand, Nefarian class-call policies
and flanks. Added subgroup-elected native fire-resistance provider, native
owned Onyxia Scale Cloak equip, Wild Magic Ice Block, and separated Wyrmguard
tank lanes/ranged clearance. No free cloaks, direct aura grants, forced object
state or marking permissions bypass. A human resistance provider is observed
and never controlled. Full walking routes must clear local lane exclusions.

AQ20: donor's active strategy is Ossirian crystal use. Retained our native
crystal lifecycle/interaction, Moam mana drain, Ayamiss rescue/add targeting
and other existing boss escape/target rules. Added ordinary ranged distance,
spacing and spare-tank melee positioning for its boss whitelist.

ZG/AQ40: retained native Thekal trio balancing, Hakkar poison, Jin'do visibility
and add targeting, Twin Emperor physical/caster targeting, Viscidus pure-frost
and shatter admission, C'thun rotating beam, native boss cast escape and other
existing policies. Added ordinary formation rules. No nonexistent donor module
is claimed, and no additional native boss script lifecycle changes are included.

Naxx: retained native Heigan dance, Noth teleport phases, Maexxna rescue,
Anub swarm escape, Grobbulus injection/cloud escape, Gluth Decimate targeting,
Sapphiron cover, Thaddius charge separation and Kel'Thuzad burst handling.
Added add priorities, safe flank/ranged formation, Grobbulus tank circuit,
Gluth boss/chow lanes and learned slows, native Tranq and specific dispels,
Thaddius ledge transition through normal validated jump machinery, KT native
Frostbolt interrupts, Loatheb five-bot spore assembly with tank/healer exclusion
and damage admission, and four melee Horsemen corners/mark relief/healer lanes.
Far Horsemen are obtained from the native active instance roster and approached
with bounded walking legs, not a fake teleport or a changed threat table.

Classic control differs from Wrath: Faerlina's 16506 Worshipper uses native
possessed Widow's Embrace, not an uncontrolled Wrath sacrifice. 16505 Followers
are killed first. Razuvious uses priests and native 16803 Understudies, not
Wrath obedience crystals. Learned native Shield Wall (29061) and Taunt (29060)
alternate on actual aura/victim/readiness state; only one owned student is
elected to spend its wall. Owned worshippers must actually arrive inside native
Embrace radius/LOS. Native ownership, cooldown and CheckCast remain decisive.
Managed autocast is disabled through ordinary ownership-checked packets once
per charm. Healers can heal owned students. Damage/AoE guards reserve available
Classic control actors without blocking friendly healing or native tank taunts.

Boundaries and verification
--------------------------
The new raid whitelist excludes map 409. No MC-specific source, Garr or Geddon
routine is changed. Existing encounter emergency actions retain precedence;
ordinary positioning preserves active casts/channels. Death, transfer, changed
map/instance, real players and invalid charm ownership reject stale work.
Native floor, full path, LOS, spell range and inventory admission remain in use.
No SQL, addon protocol, recruitment identity/ownership or GM command change.

New regression fixtures execute actual policy/admission/controller bodies with
native-shaped mocks and refusal cases. They are not live raid simulations.
Existing native CTest plus focused strategy regressions and native compilation
are required before staging. This document does not assert live clears or that
every boss has a complete donor-specific routine. Deployment, restart and live
performance verification are separate release steps and receipts.
