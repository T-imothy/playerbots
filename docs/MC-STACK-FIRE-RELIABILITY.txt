MC stack, pack damage and Magmadar positioning

Scope: shared playerbots, Classic/TBC/Wrath. The global scheduler, Pull,
Attack, follow, summon and recruitment behavior is unchanged by this release.

Regular Core Hounds (11671) and Lava Surgers (12101) require the tank stack.
Hunters no longer reserve minimum shooting distance there. Their native
switch-to-melee trigger applies even when another tank holds the target;
switching back to ranged is blocked while that encounter stack is required.
Once the stack requirement ends, normal ranged restoration is available.
Volley and melee attacks still require their native range/resource checks.
Ancient Core Hound patrols (11673) retain their separate maximum-range policy.

Regular hound damage targets the highest-health member of the engaged native
pack. A pull/attack/mark on a <=20% dog does not keep it selected while another
dog is above 20%. Above that threshold, two percentage points of hysteresis
avoid unnecessary swing churn. Assist and area target values share this choice.
Collapsed hounds (native 19951) are excluded from the AoE admission blockers;
they are not counted as attackable targets and actual crowd control/unpulled
neighbours remain protected. Native collapse/reignite mechanics are unchanged.

Flame Imps (11669): retain the opening AoE threat wait, tank allocation and
aggro-carrier return to tanks. Allocation includes all observed engaged imp
packs, including a double pull. Melee stations clear the native ten-yard
Fire Nova radius plus both combat reaches and a movement margin.
In MC, Challenging Shout/Roar are reserved for recovering at least two loose
imps. One eligible ready tank owns the overlapping attempt; an active native
taunt aura prevents the next tank from spending its cooldown simultaneously.
Native cooldown expiry/readiness allows subsequent recovery. Unpulled or
controlled neighbours within the taunt area prevent the attempt. Single-target
taunts remain available for one loose imp. Tank survival spells become eligible
below 60% health through the existing native support dispatcher.

Magmadar (11982): ranged damage/healing stations use the native learned spell
range, including range talents and combat reaches. Frost and Frost/Arcane
Classic PvE builds choose Frostbolt. Explicit Frost strategies take precedence
for range selection; Classic Arcane Missiles is suppressed for these Frost
rotations except against native frost-immune targets. Genuine Arcane builds,
later expansion rotations and native immunity rules remain available.

Ranged stations search the complete rear semicircle when a preferred lane is
blocked, aim for eight yards between ranged players and require at least five.
An already safe maximum-range station is retained. No path/LOS/terrain bypass
is used. The tank keeps an established facing/holding point through fear and
relocates nearby when ground fire covers the tank, intended boss centre or rear
melee station. Cached frontal stations are invalidated when the boss turns.

Lava Bomb 177704 / Conflagration 19428 uses its live native damage footprint,
with an additional three-yard margin and the bot's body radius. Ground paths
and cast admission continue to use the same live hazard list. Conflagration is
not dispellable. Panic 19408 is dispellable and the tank receives dispel priority.
Fear Ward and Berserker Rage remain learned, native spells. Stance preparation
requires an actual observed Panic cast and a ready learned Rage; defensive
stance is restored after the protection is active. No fixed fear timer or
manufactured immunity is used. Native fear can still prevent a stance change.

Verification executes actual policy/action bodies in all three expansion
configurations, then compiles and runs the complete per-core CTest suites.
Controlled boundary tests do not establish a live raid clear, native spell hit,
client combat meter result or full terrain simulation. Deployment and world
restart evidence are recorded separately from source/test completion.
