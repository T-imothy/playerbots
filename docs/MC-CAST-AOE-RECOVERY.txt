MC cast, formation and Classic mage AoE recovery

Observed: tanks could pull while a caster had no native combat bit yet. MC
formation admission required that bit, and Execute recomputed a different
station after GetPlan had admitted a cached station. Movement reactions also
inherited unconditional movement interruption and interrupted casts for any
formation distance over 1.5 yards. These paths can cause delayed stacking,
route stutter and repeated cancelled casts independently of the leader's DPS.

Changes shared by Classic, TBC and Wrath:
- Actual live, engaged enemy/group-victim checks admit MC formations before
  each companion has personally dealt damage. No native combat bit is forced.
- Execute uses the admitted, revalidated station and retains a native spline
  already heading there. It owns route replacement; reaction discovery does
  not pre-emptively stop movement.
- Ordinary formation movement waits for a native cast/channel to finish.
  Aggro carriers, bombs, real hazards and Magmadar's frontal danger can still
  interrupt. This applies to every class, including heals and Slam. Instant
  abilities have no cast to cancel; route reuse prevents repeated movement
  from monopolizing their action opportunities.
- Native fear ending causes one owning-map wake/cache invalidation if a real
  party combat anchor remains. Native fear, spell/GCD and control rules stay.
- Magmadar off-tanks use melee stations regardless of ranged strategy flags;
  post-fear stations are recomputed at usable range rather than retained just
  because an old point is behind the boss.
- A recoverable loose Flame Imp requires a real group victim (or group-owned
  pet) and a living same-instance tank able to react within 30 yards. Pressure
  releases opening/stand-off waits and allows ranged AoE during recovery.
  All-tank-held imps retain the initial ten-second opening. Unpulled/CC mobs,
  invalid victims, unrelated groups and absent/disabled tanks remain blocked.
  Carriers try nearest safe tank routes; tank assignments prefer their own
  slot but can recover a substantially closer imp. Native taunts remain.

Classic mage policy only:
- Controlled safe clusters of at least two support spell cleave. Native spell
  rank, range, mana, cooldown, GCD, immunity and CC checks are delegated to the
  existing actions, including their cast/channel duration.
- Imps and Ancient Core Hound mixed pulls select Blizzard from range. The
  chooser never creates a close cleave station for either.
- Other safe close clusters: apply Flamestrike when the mage's own live field
  does not affect the cluster, then Cone of Cold when at least two eligible
  enemies are actually in front and in radius, then repeat Arcane Explosion.
  Distant clusters use Blizzard while approaching a validated station.
- Hound/Surger mandatory tank stacks and health-balanced hound selection stay
  authoritative. Separate Geddon, Shazzrah, Magmadar, Golemagg, Majordomo and
  Ragnaros routines are not replaced by close cleave. Banish/sheep and native
  threat/resource checks remain intact. Later-era spec rotations are retained.
- Generic emergency Frost Nova yields on mandatory stacks and safe controlled
  cleave formations; otherwise its high-priority root/fallback can dominate
  the area damage rotation merely because raid tanks have grouped enemies.

Verification: actual production methods compiled against controlled native
interfaces in all three era configurations, full recruitment/summon/shared
combat and encounter suites, plus complete server builds/CTest. An old-source
comparison must fail the routine cast-interruption regression. These checks
do not prove a live forty-player raid clear or a damage target after restart.
No SQL, lockout, addon, ownership, teleport command or preparation wire change.
