Classic battleground objective and travel policy

Scope
  These additions are enabled by MANGOSBOT_ZERO. Autonomous matches are a
  local test configuration, not a production feature enabled by this change.
  World/dungeon/raid movement and other expansions keep their existing policy.

Travel and start
  An active battleground transition or resurrection discards an obsolete
  action wait. Accepted casts/channels are retained. Mounting has priority
  over objective travel, subject to native outdoor, combat, flag-carrier,
  water and known-mount checks. Mounting is skipped near an objective.
  Long movement splines continue, but the BG AI checks again within one second.

Arathi Basin
  The first 90 seconds after gates open distribute 15 bots as follows:
  home flag 2, Blacksmith 5, Lumber Mill 4, Gold Mine 4. Both factions use
  the same distribution; Stables/Farm are substituted as their home flags.
  Later, a small guard stays at owned/assaulted nodes; mobile squads retake
  threatened nodes and attack others. Assignments use sorted team bot GUIDs,
  retaining dead teammates so that every death does not reroll the team.

Alterac Valley
  Six of 40 bots work on towers, three handle threatened home objectives,
  and the main force advances through the enemy graveyard chain. Pending
  graveyard assaults retain a pair of guards while the force advances.
  The tower squad assaults together and leaves a guard on each pending tower.
  Offensive teams use separate midfield approaches from existing AV paths,
  with native navigation and combat checks. The actual banner remains the
  objective; route legs cannot become capture or combat objectives themselves.
  Towers are approached at the live banner position, including its floor.
  Main-force survivors help unfinished towers after the graveyard chain.
  The general becomes the shared objective once all four enemy towers are
  destroyed and the final graveyard is fully captured. An assault in progress
  is not treated as a completed tower or graveyard. Native timers and NPC
  strength remain unchanged; this does not guarantee a fixed match duration.

Capture and combat
  AB/AV captures use native spell 21651 on active lock-1479 banners. They
  preserve normal cast time, ownership, immunity, range and static-map LOS
  validation. The banner's own model is not treated as an occluding wall.
  A teammate's cast only suppresses another capture at that same banner.
  The bot fights immediate blockers and attacks on itself/nearby teammates,
  or enemies at its objective. Unrelated distant PvP targets do not override
  the objective. Explicit human-master behavior retains its existing checks.

Reputation
  The reward rules and multipliers are unchanged. AB reputation comes from
  resource thresholds, WSG from captures, and AV from its native objective,
  NPC and completion rewards. Failed captures can therefore also stop gains.

Diagnostics
  The existing explicit debug why command reports BG status and timing.
  In AB/AV it includes at most six nearby native capture banners: entry,
  distance, event/node state, spawn/use state, LOS (full and static), and
  player usability/cast state. This produces no new per-tick live log stream.

Regression coverage
  classic_battleground_policy: mirrored opening allocation, objective bounds,
  defense/attack split, completed-tower general gate, native class layout
  across module/core compilation, and actual gate/respawn wait handling.
  classic_banner: actual capture action, native cast rejection, walls versus
  banner models, immunity/transfer/cast guards, and teammate same-flag checks.
  classic_alterac_objectives: actual AV planner with mirrored faction states,
  banner coordinates, pending capture guards and final-general conditions.
  Existing WSG and shared BG recovery regressions cover all expansion modes.
