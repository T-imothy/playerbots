Raid and dungeon engagement audit, October 13, 2026

Scope

The shared Engine retry correction applies on every map in Classic, TBC and
Wrath: a failed autonomous MovementAction is retried when its bot/target
coordinates or native movement permission change. Unchanged failures remain
bounded. Explicit player commands still bypass autonomous failure throttling.
Native resource, proc, stance, GCD, cooldown and casting rules remain intact.

All existing encounter position actions were checked for the same arrival
starvation pattern. They used movement flags or a retained generator as proof
that travel remained unfinished. Those tests could repeatedly select a stop
reaction at an already reached station. EncounterMovementRequired now tests
distance and the live unfinished spline's endpoint. The positional hold still
exists in its multiplier; a completed arrival no longer consumes combat frames.

Additional MC corrections

Safe ground-fire escape releases its movement hold as soon as the bot is clear,
including before the normal cached-value refresh. Native hazard route admission
still blocks re-entry/crossing. A validated useful formation may take an actor
directly out of a ground-only hazard; bombs, pulses and death bursts keep their
independent holds. Required pack stacks are preserved when ground fire overlaps.

A non-tank's ordinary rear station follows its actual hostile current target;
it cannot hold melee at a different trash mob during a multiboss pull. Existing
mandatory pack/carrier/banish rules keep precedence. Current tank ownership and
loose-mob pickup still outrank a cached gathering station.

Magmadar's accepted formation tracks boss relocation, facing, final native 3D
spell range and spell LOS. Hunters remain ranged outside required stack packs.
Bounded shorter-range alternatives allow walls/ledges without per-frame
replanning of an accepted fallback. Actual ground-fire overlap can interrupt a
cast to escape; routine formation correction preserves a valid cast/channel.
Fear recovery waits for native combat control to return before consuming its
one-shot wakeup. No fear aura, stun, native spell immunity or cooldown is bypassed.

Firelord/Lava Spawn hunter/warlock stations use native learned spell ranges,
range talents/reach, LOS, full native path/hazard/unpulled-pack validation and
a bounded candidate search. They do not persist into a required hound/Surger
stack or depend on the leader beginning DPS.

Warrior role audit

The reported Tadryn screenshot shows Shield Slam/Revenge and substantial
incoming damage: it demonstrates a tank rotation, not a Fury rotation test.
Native roles use configured tank strategies/talent tab, not merely knowledge
of talent spells (bots can know abilities from more than one tab). Fury/Arms
schedulers, melee reach, native stance/proc/resource guards, Whirlwind/cleave,
shout refresh, and the other classes' cast/channel paths are covered by the
existing class/warrior and shared timing regressions. The movement/hold fixes
restore scheduling opportunity; no damage multiplier or artificial rage is added.

Debuffs

Classic bot DebuffTrigger used an arbitrary 16-aura cutoff; TBC used 40; Wrath
already returned no cutoff. All now defer to native spell application/stacking.
The server aura-holder maps and modifier application are independent of visible
client slots in all three cores. Native field sizes and aura slot serializers
were not enlarged. Old clients can display fewer effects than are active.
Existing aura/rank/caster ownership, immunity and native spell checks are retained.

Verification and limits

Firewalker healing and mixed trash

The actual living, hostile Firewalker victim identifies the threatened group
tank. Injured Firewalker tanks receive the existing class fast-heal actions;
critically injured allies retain the first urgency band. Regrowth/Riptide direct
healing is not blocked by an existing HoT, and valid tank heals are not clipped
at the ordinary almost-full-health threshold. Full health and native spell
admission remain authoritative. No damage, health or mana values are fabricated.

Lava Annihilator joins Lava Elemental/Reaver only in the existing engaged mixed
trash Banish coordinator. The release ledger and one-at-a-time cleanup remain.
Unaccompanied Annihilator/Surger pulls do not receive perpetual Banish. Without
a priority skull, Firewalkers precede Flameguards. Both fire mobs retain native
Banish immunity. Garr's allocation, tank lanes and post-boss releases are unchanged.

Majordomo and Ragnaros

Majordomo mages retain native Polymorph ownership, including human-owned sheep,
and use an already legal casting station before requesting movement. The sheep
support action precedes optional dispels/buffs. Completed/immune assignments
release their old positioning hold immediately. Native four-add-death Polymorph
immunity is retained; it cannot be bypassed to promise indefinite sheep control
after all four elites die. Only bot mages are automated; a human mage is not made
to cast or move. Elite focus and one-at-a-time healer focus remain coordinated.

After a real knockback/jump landing, bots defer until native combat control
returns, then wake once, clearing MC position caches without cancelling a new
cast or altering spell/GCD cooldowns. This wake applies to every map/expansion.
Spare Ragnaros tanks now use the rear melee attack station, rather than remaining
permanently outside attack range. A live Wrath cast still temporarily overrides
that station. The bounded recovery search admits additional rear dry-ground
stations through native endpoint/path/trajectory safety; it does not walk across
an unvalidated route or teleport. Sons/submerge formations remain separate.

Long-duration group buffs

Fortitude/Prayer, Spirit/Prayer, Shadow Protection/Prayer, Intellect/Brilliance
and Mark/Gift are checked as equivalent families at target selection and again
before the automatic mutation. Any still-active equivalent aura prevents rebuff.
Available strongest native providers divide raid subgroups; dead, transferring,
disabled, out-of-map/range or resource-blocked providers drop out. Native pending
player/bot casts are respected. Group variants require at least three eligible
missing players in their expansion's native scope, otherwise single buffs fill
the gaps. Shields, HoTs, totems, cooldowns and native paladin blessing ownership
retain their own policies. Native mana/reagents/range/LOS/GCD are never bypassed.

Geddon patrol startup

The earlier absence occurred before his later kill and is not explained away
by that later DONE save. All three databases contain a native 17-point template
route, but the spawn lacked active-object status. Instance entry now loads only
the bounded existing waypoint route and activates the living native boss; death
deactivates it. Completed saves/respawns, path coordinates, combat AI and loot
remain native and unchanged. There is no kill-trash spawn threshold or SQL edit.
