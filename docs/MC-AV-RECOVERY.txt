MC / AV engagement recovery, 2026-10-13

Findings and changes

1. The hunter's mandatory Core Hound/Surger stack correctly ends with the
   native pack. A nearby single Firelord lacked an encounter ranged station,
   however, and raid flee is intentionally disabled. Native shots could stay
   TOO_CLOSE without a usable positioning action. Hunters and warlocks now
   plan bounded native-range stations for tank-held Firelords/Lava Spawns.
   Pack stacking still takes precedence. Admission checks current target,
   native engagement, same group/instance, CC, native range, terrain, LOS,
   ground hazards and unpulled-pack routes. This does not re-enable fleeing.

2. Autonomous movement failure readiness used an empty signature. A failed
   reach could remain throttled after either endpoint moved or native control
   returned. Live failure entries now compare endpoint positions and movement
   control. Unchanged failed routes retain bounded retry. Spell resource/GCD
   checks and explicit-command behavior remain unchanged.

3. Ground fire's approach band could produce an exclusive stationary hold
   instead of Magmadar formation. Formation execution also rejected nearby
   fire without checking whether its destination and route were clear.
   A valid Magmadar station can now take precedence for ground fire, with
   every native route guard retained. Actual fire overlap interrupts casts;
   ordinary formation corrections still wait for casts/channels to finish.
   Bombs and other encounter hazards retain their separate escape policy.

4. A cached Magmadar station could outlive boss relocation. Plans now retain
   the boss position used to admit them and refresh after displacement, a
   facing change, lost LOS or invalid spell reach. Accepted refreshed plans
   are cached, so a stationary boss does not force repeated path searches.
   Blocked maximum-range lanes can use nearer legal lanes, with a total
   bound of 32 native path checks and five-yard minimum ranged spacing.
   Native three-dimensional range and LOS are checked after height adjustment.
   Hunters remain a ranged role here even after the preceding melee stack.
   The holding tank's existing stable facing/fire relocation remains active.

5. Fear aura removal can precede native control recovery. The one-shot wake
   now waits for CanReactInCombat rather than losing the recovery signal in
   that interval. No fear aura, immunity, cooldown or native control is changed.

6. Battleground attackers omit normal group aggregation. An unmarked AV NPC
   fighting a nearby teammate was therefore absent unless it also fought the
   bot. AV now admits an enemy NPC already engaged with the same active native
   BG raid. BG identity/team, world/instance/phase, distance, CC and native
   attackability remain authoritative. Selection alone cannot pull an NPC.
   Skull precedence remains; unmarked focus mode only falls back to these
   engaged AV NPCs. Enemy-player PvP policy and other BGs remain unchanged.

Verification

movement_failure_readiness_regression.py executes the actual retry methods.
alterac_engagement_regression.py executes actual attacker admission/discovery.
molten_core_support_regression.py exercises native-shaped Firelord stations,
pack transitions, tank relocation, blocked paths and maximum-range fallback.
molten_core_position_regression.py exercises actual Calculate/GetPlan/Execute
with nearby/overlapping fire, boss movement, cached fallback and native control.
mc_rotation_recovery_regression.py exercises actual cast/escape/fear recovery.
Each runs under Classic, TBC and Wrath defines. Old reviewed bodies deliberately
fail the movement, unmarked AV, fire-hold and native-control recovery cases.

These are controlled-interface regression tests, not a live 40-bot encounter.
The existing bounded live logs confirmed hunter TOO_CLOSE failures and ranged
reach failures, but did not attribute every warlock decision to an individual.
The AV clip was decoded locally (176x144, about 10 seconds); unreadable names
or spell bars were not used as evidence. Source findings are distinct from a
claim that every observed live failure has been independently reproduced.
