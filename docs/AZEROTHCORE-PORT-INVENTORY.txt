CURRENT CRITICAL RELEASE:
- Adopted the upstream full-family buff check and group-variant threshold concept using native CMaNGOS APIs, expansion-specific scope and provider fallback.
- Kept working Garr and Geddon combat; no replacement imported. Geddon startup activation follows the existing native waypoint route.
- Shared combat/movement recovery and AV already-engaged NPC admission remain CMaNGOS-native and apply across the relevant expansion targets.
- Magmadar in this upstream revision has resistance support plus an anti-fear TODO; no fear/fire recovery routine to substitute for our native repair.
- Majordomo has shadow resistance support, not sheep assignment/release coordination; our repaired native coordination is retained.
- Existing native dungeon/raid movement handlers now release a completed station instead of starving casts behind retained motion flags.

NOT COMPLETED BY THIS RELEASE:
A complete import of every listed raid/dungeon/battleground handler has not been implemented or verified.
Each remaining port requires native creature/spell/phase verification per available expansion, role/target/movement adapters, wiring and regression tests.
AzerothCore is Wrath-based. Its Onyxia/Naxx difficulty IDs, vehicles, spell APIs, threat APIs and dungeon availability are not interchangeable with Classic/TBC.
No blind replacement, no immunity changes, no copied force-cast/teleport shortcuts and no live database edits are included.
